## Creating Custom Scenarios

When creating your own scenarios, consider:

1. **Context**: Be specific about the situation
2. **Target Audience**: Define who you're talking to (role, background, expectations)
3. **Purpose**: What skills are you practicing?
4. **Language**: What language should be used?
5. **Custom Prompt**: Add any specific AI behavior instructions

### Tips for Effective Scenarios

- **Be Specific**: The more detailed your scenario, the better the AI can role-play
- **Set Clear Goals**: Define what you want to improve
- **Match Reality**: Use realistic scenarios you'll encounter
- **Vary Difficulty**: Start easy and progress to challenging scenarios
- **Include Context**: Add relevant background information
